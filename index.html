How to Play

Goal: Build a thriving metropolis. Balance power, water, and happiness to grow population.

Controls

  • Select a building from the sidebar.
  • Click on the green grid to place it.
  • Click 'End Turn' to collect taxes and proceed.

Tips:

  • Houses need Power and Water.
  • Factories earn money but reduce happiness.
  • Unlock new maps when you have enough cash.
